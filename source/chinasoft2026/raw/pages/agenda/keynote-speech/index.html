特邀报告嘉宾简介

1. 特邀报告：孙凝晖 院士

题目：智能时代处理器设计范式的变革

摘要：报告梳理处理器设计范式演进与历史路线之争，指出智能时代需从追求性能（频率、功耗、面积）PPA转向兼顾性能与敏捷度，以更快设计应对多样性。报告将介绍在探索处理器新范式方面的实践，包括面向对象体系结构、AI自动设计芯片、验证智能体、开源硬件及香山/RISC-V生态进展等。

简介：孙凝晖，中国工程院院士。中国科学院计算技术研究所研究员。计算机系统结构专家，主要从事高性能计算机研究。曾担任中国科学院计算技术研究所所长，中国计算机学会理事长。先后参加了曙光一号并行计算机，曙光1000大规模并行机的研制，领导了曙光2000-6000高性能计算机、新型高通量计算机和专用智能计算机的研制。获得多项国家科技奖励。

2. 特邀报告：云晓春 院士

题目：大模型编程突破性进展下软件漏洞的发展态势

摘要：今年以来，大模型编程取得了突破性进展，漏洞的规模、类型与发现方式，正在被 AI 同步重构，但漏洞的本质仍是“设计与实现的缺陷”，AI 没有改变安全的底层逻辑。真正的风险，不是 AI 写出了有漏洞的代码；而是我们用它写代码的速度，超过了我们理解与治理它的速度。因此，决定安全水位的不是 AI 本身，而是“治理速度”能否追上“技术速度”。防守方已握有同等工具 — 主动布局者，将把这次变革转化为安全能力的跃升。

简介：云晓春，中国工程院院士，网络空间安全技术专家，现任中关村实验室首席科学家。获中国青年科技奖、首届国家网络安全优秀人才奖，入选国家“万人计划”领军人才、全国优秀科技工作者。他作为第一完成人获得国家科技进步一等奖1项、二等奖2项，第一发明人获授权发明专利45项，主持制定国家及通信行业标准15项，出版专著2部，发表学术论文100余篇，培养博士研究生30余人。他长期致力于国家网络空间安全的技术研究和工程实践，主持研制了国家网络安全系列平台，主导建立了国家网络安全应急处置技术体系，为有效应对全球性网络安全威胁做出了重要贡献。

3. 特邀报告：Carlo Ghezzi 院士

题目：Responsible Software Engineering for AI-Enabled Software Systems

摘要：Software is the defining technology of the new world in which we live: it manages and controls the integration of the digital and physical spheres with human. Through software, humans have been able to automate well-defined tasks. Through AI, software is now populating the world with new entities that have agency: they can learn, adapt, evolve, and act autonomously.

The talk mainly revolves around these questions: Is software engineering as a discipline up to the responsibilities demanded by the crucial role of software has in the world? Does the practice of software development informed by them? Is software engineering education preparing the future engineers with needed competences? Despite their profound impact on humans, society, and the environment software systems are still engineered as “neutral” artifacts to be constructed through purely technical skills. This, however, is threatening their human and social sustainability. Inspired by the principles of Digital Humanism, the talk elaborates on the notion of “Responsible software engineering” as an answer to the question of making software engineers aware of their crucial role in shaping the future world and directing systems development in the direction of serving and benefiting society.

简介：Carlo Ghezzi is an Emeritus Professor at Politecnico di Milano, Italy, where he has been active for more than 50 years as a researcher, faculty member, and educator. Among other roles, he has been founding Chair of Software Engineering, Department Chair, Member of the Academic Senate, Vice-Rector for Research, Chair of the Ethical Committee. He holds an Honorary Doctor Degree from the Technical University of Vienna.

He is an ACM Fellow, IEEE Fellow, member of Academia Europaea, member of the Italian academy Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere. He has been awarded the ACM SIGSOFT Outstanding Research Award, the ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Service Award, the IEEE TCSE Distinguished Education Award, the Leloir Prize by the Government of Argentina for International Cooperation in Science, Technology and Innovation. He has been on the advisory board of several international research programs and institutions in Europe, China, Japan, and the USA. He has been President of Informatics Europe, the association of computer science departments and research laboratories in Europe.

Carlo Ghezzi has been Program Co-Chair and General chair of several prestigious conferences (including the two flagship conferences on Software Engineering, ICSE and ESEC) and member of the program committee of many international conferences. He has been Editor in Chief of the ACM Trans. on Software Engineering and Methodology, Associate Editor of Communications of the ACM, IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, Science of Computer Programming.

Ghezzi's research has been focusing on software engineering and programming languages. He co-authored over 200 papers and 15 books, and coordinated several national and international research projects. He was awarded an Advanced Grant from the ERC (European Research Council). He is currently a Steering Committee member of the Digital Humanism Initiative (https://caiml.org/dighum/).
